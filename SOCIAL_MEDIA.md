## Released: Hemmingway Writing Assistant v0.0.4 (2026-09-21)

### LinkedIn
Hemmingway Writing Assistant v0.0.4 for Amplenote is officially live! 🖋️⚡

Most AI writing tools do one of two things: they either aggressively overwrite your entire note with generic, robotic filler, or they trap you behind a single rigid prompt. We built the Hemmingway Writing Assistant to change that.

Powered by the hemmingway-27b open-weights model (262k context), this plugin transforms rough thoughts, bullet fragments, and robotic drafts into crisp, engaging, unmistakable human prose—chunk by chunk, with complete author control.

What's New in v0.0.4:

• 7 Tailored Editorial Presets: Switch effortlessly between De-AIify (strips generic cliches and robotic transitions like 'delve' or 'testament'), The Hemingway Edit (cuts weak adverbs and passive voice), Rough Notes to Polished Prose, Executive Brief, Storytelling, and Clean Grammar Only.
• 4 Interactive Diff View Modes: Inspect suggestions your way with Clean Prose reading view, Unified Inline Diff, Side-by-Side synchronized comparison, or tabular Changes Only.
• Universal Sandboxed-Safe In-DOM Modals: Replaced all restricted browser prompts with custom, full-screen expandable in-DOM dialogs for manual edits, custom prompt injections, and reason-guided re-reviews.
• Production-Grade Test Suite: 50 unit and integration tests across 10 suites covering tokenizers, Myers LCS diff algorithms, session persistence, and API mocks with 100% pass rate.
• Complete Privacy & Stale Overwrite Protection: In-progress work auto-saves to localStorage, and external note updates are automatically detected before committing changes.

**Title:** Hemmingway Writing Assistant v0.0.4 for Amplenote — De-AIify drafts & polish rough notes into crisp human prose with 4 diff modes and 50 automated tests

**Post:**
Hey everyone!

I just released **v0.0.4** of the **Hemmingway Writing Assistant Plugin for Amplenote**.

If you use AI to draft notes or brainstorm ideas, you've probably noticed how quickly writing turns into monotonous, predictable corporate soup: *"It's a testament to the intricate tapestry of delving into..."*

This plugin integrates the specialized **`hemmingway-27b`** model (262k context) directly inside Amplenote to help you polish drafts, prune bloated sentences, and convert rough meeting dumps into engaging, clear narrative prose.

### Highlights & Key Features:

1. **7 Purpose-Built Editorial Presets**:
   - **Make It Sound Human (De-AIify)**: Eliminates formulaic sentence structures, sycophantic buzzwords, and repetitive transitions.
   - **The Hemingway Edit**: Prunes weak adverbs, converts passive voice to active, and shortens rambling clauses.
   - **Rough Notes ➔ Polished Prose**: Connects fragmented bullet thoughts into cohesive paragraphs.
   - **Executive Brief**: High-signal, action-oriented summaries for decision-makers.
   - **Simplify & Clarify**: Demystifies convoluted technical jargon into plain English.
   - **Storytelling & Vivid Narrative**: Enriches pacing and cadence.
   - **Clean Grammar & Typos Only**: Precise copyediting that respects your original phrasing.

2. **4 Interactive Diff View Modes**:
   - `✨ Clean Prose`: Readably rendered rewritten prose with polished additions highlighted in soft emerald green.
   - `🔀 Inline Diff`: Unified line-level diff with `<del>` and `<ins>`.
   - `👥 Side-by-Side`: Dual-pane synchronized scrolling comparison.
   - `📋 Changes Only`: Tabular item-by-item breakdown of changes.

3. **100% Sandboxed In-DOM UI Architecture**:
   - Native browser `window.prompt()` is blocked inside Amplenote's iframe sandbox. We built custom, expandable in-DOM dialogs for manual editing (`✏️ Edit`), targeted re-prompting (`🔄 Re-Review`), and custom prompts (`+ Custom`).

4. **Reliability & State Safety**:
   - Stale note overwrite guard alerts you if a note was modified externally before saving.
   - `localStorage` session state persistence keeps your in-progress work safe across page refreshes.
   - 50 automated tests across 10 Jest test suites verifying Myers LCS diff algorithms, tokenizers, state machines, and API clients.

The plugin is 100% free and open-source under GPL-3.0.
